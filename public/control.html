Order of service

Line the service up before Sunday. Put something on screen (a verse, a name, a message…) and press + Add to order under the preview, or press + Add song to order on the Songs tab for a whole song. During the service, Next ▶ (or N) goes through it in order. Songs go section by section, then move on.

📱 Use on your phone

Scan with a phone on the same Wi-Fi to open this panel on it. If it doesn't open, allow Node.js through Windows Firewall (Private networks).

🎛 Stream Deck and remote buttons

In Stream Deck, use the Website action, tick GET request in background, and paste one of these. Next and Previous work on whichever tab is open here (verses, notes, songs or the order), so keep this panel open in OBS.
